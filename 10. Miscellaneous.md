# 1. Score Board

**Definition:**

> The Score Board page is a hidden feature in OWASP Juice Shop that tracks the completion status of challenges. Finding it demonstrates your ability to explore and analyze the application’s frontend and source code.

**Steps to Access:**

* Open the **Dashboard Page**.
* Press **Ctrl+U** to view the page **source code**.
* Search for **`main.js`** and locate the endpoint for the Score Board.

```html
http://10.30.30.145:3000/#/score-board
```

# 2. Privacy Policy

**Definition:**

> The Privacy Policy page in OWASP Juice Shop provides information about how user data is handled. It may also contain hidden challenges or clues, such as insecure endpoints, hidden comments, or hints for other challenges. Exploring it tests your ability to inspect the frontend and discover subtle vulnerabilities.

**Steps to Access:**

1. Log in to your account.
2. Navigate to **Privacy & Security → Privacy Policy**.
> **Alternatively:-**
1. open the **Dashboard Page** and click the **Privacy Policy** link in the footer or menu.
2. Press **Ctrl+U** to view the page **source code** for hidden hints or endpoints.

```html
http://10.30.30.145:3000/#/privacy-security/privacy-policy
```
