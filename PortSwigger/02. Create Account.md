# How to Create an Account on PortSwigger

1. **Go to Website**

   * Open your browser and visit: [https://portswigger.net](https://portswigger.net)
   * Or directly go to: [https://portswigger.net/web-security](https://portswigger.net/web-security)

2. **Click on Sign Up / Register**

   * On the top-right corner, you will see **"Login / Sign up"**.
   * Click on it.

3. **Fill Registration Form**

   * Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name**
   * Enter a valid **Email Address** (use Gmail or any working mail)
   * Create a **Password** (choose strong one)

4. **Agree to Terms**

   * Tick the box to agree with PortSwigger’s terms and privacy policy.

5. **Verify Email**

   * After submitting, PortSwigger will send a verification link to your email.
   * Open your inbox, click the link, and your account will be activated.

6. **Login and Start Learning**

   * Now go to [Web Security Academy](https://portswigger.net/web-security)
   * Login with your new account.
   * You can now access labs, save progress, and track learning.


### Benefits of Creating an Account

* Saves your progress in labs.
* Lets you track difficulty levels (Apprentice → Practitioner → Expert → Master).
* Gives you access to certificates when you finish certain paths.
